Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Effective Date: September 1, 2026

🛡️ Our Core Commitment to Privacy

SmartDine POS (also operating under SmartBizz), engineered by DevMonks (devmonks.space), is designed with a Local-First, Zero-Cloud-Cost architecture. We believe your restaurant sales records, customer invoices, and menu pricing belong entirely to you.

We do not sell personal or business data, we do not track user location, and dine-in guest table QR ordering requires zero customer phone number or account registration.

1. Scope & Applicability

This Privacy Policy applies to the SmartDine POS Android Application distributed via the Google Play Store, the SmartDine Digital Table QR Ordering Web Portal (located at /r/), and related web services hosted on smartdine-pos.web.app and smartbizz.devmonks.space.

2. Information We Handle & Where It Is Stored

Unlike conventional cloud POS platforms that ingest your operational data into external advertising or telemetry networks, SmartDine keeps your records strictly isolated:

Category of Data What Is Handled Where It Is Stored Purpose
Store Profile Data Restaurant Name, GSTIN / Tax Identification, Address, Contact Phone, and Merchant UPI ID. Locally on device (Hive key-value store) & in your private Google Drive Spreadsheet. Generating GST-compliant thermal tax invoices and displaying restaurant branding on digital table menus.
Sales & Dining Receipts Items ordered, table number, timestamps, bill totals, payment method (Cash, UPI QR, Card Swipe). Locally on till tablet; synced directly to the restaurant owner's connected Google Sheet. Kitchen preparation, till settlement, shift closure (Z-Report), and accounting reconciliation.
Hardware Device Identity Anonymized Hardware Device ID / Android ID. Central licensing database (smartdine-restaurant-pos). Enforcing physical device limits authorized under your restaurant's subscription tier.
Staff Credentials Staff Member Name, Assigned Role (Manager, Waiter, Cashier, Chef), and bcrypt-hashed 4-digit PIN. Encrypted in local device cache & in your private Google Sheet. Role-based access control, void audit logging, and shift attribution.
Dine-in Guests (QR Ordering) Temporary session cart items and table number. Encrypted payload routed via webhook to your kitchen display. Placing orders without requiring diners to install apps, disclose phone numbers, or create accounts.

3. Android Device Permissions Required by the App

To operate seamlessly on restaurant floors, the SmartDine Android app requests the following specific operating system permissions in full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies:

  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN): Used exclusively to discover, pair with, and transmit ESC/POS raw print commands to 58mm (2-inch) and 80mm (3-inch) wireless Bluetooth thermal receipt printers.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to verify subscription license validity, synchronize KOT orders with your Google Spreadsheet, and communicate with the Kitchen Display System (KDS).
  • Camera (CAMERA - Optional): Used solely when a staff member uses the in-app barcode/QR camera scanner to look up inventory items or read customer table standees.
  • Storage & USB Access: Used to interface with USB thermal receipt printers, trigger RJ11 cash drawer kicks, and export passphrase-protected local database backups (.backup files).

4. Google API Services & Google Drive Limited Use Disclosure

SmartDine integrates with Google Sheets and Google Drive to give store owners full ownership of their financial database. SmartDine's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We only request scopes strictly necessary to read and write your restaurant's dedicated spreadsheets (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets, drive.file).
  • We never access, read, or inspect personal emails, photos, contacts, or unrelated Google Drive files.
  • Your Google Drive data is never transferred, sold, or shared with external advertising networks, data brokers, or AI model trainers.

5. Data Security & Cryptographic Safeguards

We implement industry-standard technical measures to safeguard operations:

  • Encrypted Payload Transmission: Web ordering communication utilizes TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption and HMAC-SHA256 authenticated signatures.
  • Offline Resilience: Even during total broadband outages, all sales are secured in encrypted Hive storage on the local device.
  • Passphrase-Protected Archives: Manual data backups are encrypted using AES-256 with a security passphrase chosen by the store owner.

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

SmartDine POS is a commercial enterprise point-of-sale and business operations software application designed for commercial venues. We do not direct our services to children under the age of 13, nor do we knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Account & Data Deletion Requests

Because SmartDine stores business records in your own Google Drive, you have unconditional control to delete or archive your spreadsheets at any time.

To request the permanent removal of your organization account, license profile, or device registration records from our cloud licensing directory, please submit a request on our dedicated Data Deletion Request Page or email smartdine.platform@gmail.com. Requests are completed within 48 business hours.
