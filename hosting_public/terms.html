4. Payment settlements

SmartBizz does not process payments and is not a payment gateway. It encodes standard NPCI-compliant UPI links (such as upi://pay?pa=...) and renders them as QR codes, and it records the payments your staff confirm.

All funds transfer directly from the customer's bank account to your merchant bank account. DevMonks does not touch, hold or route your money, does not charge intermediary transaction surcharges, and is not liable for customer chargebacks or banking network downtimes. A payment a diner claims in the guest app is recorded as unconfirmed until your counter verifies it against your own bank or UPI app.

5. Offline operation & system clock integrity

SmartBizz is designed so billing can continue on the device during broadband outages. To prevent cashier fraud and maintain sequential invoice auditing, the application requires your physical tablet to maintain a valid network-synchronized system clock. Deliberately rolling back system time to backdate sales triggers tamper safeguards.
