Frequently Asked Questions & Quick Guides

1. How do I pair my Bluetooth thermal receipt printer?

SmartDine works with all standard 58mm (2-inch) and 80mm (3-inch) ESC/POS Bluetooth printers:

  • Turn on the thermal printer and ensure paper is inserted (feeding from the bottom of the roll).
  • Open Android Settings → Bluetooth and pair with the printer (default PIN is usually 0000 or 1234).
  • Open SmartDine → Settings → Thermal Printer Configuration.
  • Tap Scan Devices → select your paired printer → tap Print Test Receipt.

2. How do customers order from Table QR codes?

SmartDine does not require diners to install any apps or enter phone numbers:

  • In the POS app, open Table Management → Print Standees (PDF).
  • Print the A5 acrylic standee cards and place them on tables.
  • Customers open their standard phone camera, scan the QR, and the digital photo menu loads in under a second.
  • Orders go straight to the kitchen display screen wirelessly.

3. Does SmartDine work when the internet goes down?

Yes, 100%. SmartDine runs on local Hive databases embedded inside your physical tablet. Even during a complete broadband outage, your staff can punch KOTs, print thermal bills, open the cash drawer, and run service uninterrupted. When internet returns, all bills sync automatically to Google Sheets.
