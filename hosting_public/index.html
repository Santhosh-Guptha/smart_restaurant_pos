Questions owners ask us

Do I need to buy special POS hardware?

No. SmartDine runs on ordinary Android phones and tablets — the ones your staff already carry work fine. For printing, it pairs with the standard Bluetooth and USB receipt printers restaurants already use. Hand the kitchen any tablet and it becomes the kitchen display.

What happens when the internet goes down mid-service?

Service carries on. Orders still reach the kitchen screen, bills still print, payments are still recorded — everything is kept safely on the device and sent along the moment you're back online. Your staff see a small notice; your guests see nothing at all.

Who owns our sales and customer data?

You do, completely. Every bill, every day, lives in a form you can open and export whenever you like. If you ever leave, you take all of it with you — there's no lock-in and nothing held hostage.

Can the kitchen have separate screens for tandoor, grill and bar?

Yes. Each ticket is split by station, so the tandoor sees only tandoor items and the bar sees only drinks. Any number of screens, each showing its own work.

We have more than one outlet. Does each one need its own setup?

Each outlet gets its own tables, staff, printers and payment account — but you set the menu and prices once for the brand and see every outlet on one screen. A manager in one branch never sees another branch's till.

How long does setup take?

Most single outlets are taking orders the same afternoon: add your menu, print a QR for each table, sign the staff in, hand the kitchen a tablet. For a group rollout, talk to us — we'll plan it with you.
