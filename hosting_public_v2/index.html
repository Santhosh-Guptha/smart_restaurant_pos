            Questions

            Straight answers.

            Anything else? Ask us →

            Does it work when the internet drops?

            On Offline, billing runs on the device without depending on the cloud; the licence is re-checked online at least every 30 days. On Basic and above, the till keeps billing and catches up when the connection returns.

            Where is my data?

            On Offline, on your device, with an encrypted backup you control. On Basic and above, in your own Google Drive, one sheet per store. We collect only limited usage analytics such as bill counts.

            Do you take a cut of payments?

            No. SmartBizz is not a payment gateway. The UPI QR pays your own UPI ID, or you settle by cash, khata, or your own card machine.

            Can I sell loose goods and sizes?

            Yes. Price per kg or litre with weight entry or weighing-scale labels, and sizes and colours as variants with their own barcode, price and stock.

            Does it handle pharmacy batches and expiry?

            Yes. Batch and expiry on goods-in, first-to-expire selling, expired stock blocked at the till, and batch, expiry and MRP printed on the bill with your drug licence number.

            Can I keep my printer?

            Yes, if it is an ESC/POS thermal printer (58 or 80 mm) over Bluetooth, USB or the network.

            What is not built yet?

            Restaurant recipe inventory, sales returns and exchanges, and scanning on the counter desk (scanning is on the barcode billing desk today). We list these openly so you know before you sign up.
