SmartDine Android POS v1.0.8 (Build 9)

What's New:
1. Counter Billing POS - Pending Bills Tab:
   - Added dedicated "Pending Bills" tab with live count badge alongside "New Bill".
   - Displays all unpaid and postpaid orders (Dine-In, Takeaway, and Web QR Self-Orders).
   - Shows live elapsed time, table number, token #, and full item summary.
   - Quick search & filter chips (All, Dine-In, Takeaway, QR Web).
   - Top banner tracking total unsettled amount and unpaid count in real time.

2. Accept Payment & Settle Workflow:
   - Tap "Accept Payment" on any pending bill to view itemized breakdown and total payable.
   - Multiple payment tender modes: Cash (with quick change calculator), UPI / QR, Card, and Other.
   - Instant 1-tap "Mark Payment Done & Settle":
     - Updates order status to PAID in Firestore & local Hive.
     - Automatically clears and vacates occupied Dine-In tables.
     - Syncs settled transaction to Google Sheets "Dining Bills" tab.
     - Auto-prints customer tax invoice to connected Bluetooth thermal printer.
   - Added pre-bill print check and quick "Add Dishes" button to running table bills.
